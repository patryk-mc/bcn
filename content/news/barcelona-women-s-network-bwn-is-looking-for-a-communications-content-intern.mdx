**About the Role**

Barcelona Women’s Network (BWN) is looking for a creative, organised and proactive Communications and Content intern to support our team. Working closely with the BWN Board Members, this role will help ensure that our members and wider community stay informed, engaged and connected. We anticipate that this will require up to 25 hrs / week (some weeks may be less).

Key Responsibilities

**1. Social Media Content (60%)**

● Create and publish engaging weekly posts across LinkedIn, Instagram and Facebook, highlighting upcoming events, community activities, collaborations, new partnerships, fundraising initiatives and other BWN news.

● Maintain consistent messaging, tone of voice and visual branding across platforms.

**2. Website Content Updates (5%)**

● Assist Board Members in updating the BWN website content as needed, including adding and updating event information, announcements, images and other relevant updates.

● Ensure published information remains accurate and up to date.

**3. Monthly Events Newsletter (15%)**

● Gather event details, descriptions, images and registration information from Board members, and other relevant parties.

● Build the events newsletter using the existing template in BWN’s email platform (WildApricot).

● Ensure event information is accurate, complete and published on schedule.

**4. Monthly Member Newsletter (15%)**

● Assist Board members to gather highlights from the previous month, organisational updates and upcoming initiatives.

● Build the newsletter using the existing newsletter template in BWN’s email platform (WildApricot).

● Coordinate content reviews and approvals ahead of scheduling & distribution.

**5. Other MISC support (5%)**

● Assist Board members in other administrative tasks as needed.

Ideal Candidate

● Professional and respectful.

● Strong written and spoken English, with excellent editing and proofreading skills.

● Creative, detail-oriented and well organised, with the ability to meet deadlines.

● Comfortable creating content for social media and digital communications.

● Familiarity with G-Suite, PowerPoint, Excel, and email marketing platforms.

● Proactive, collaborative and comfortable coordinating with multiple stakeholders.

What You'll Gain

Hands-on experience in digital communications, content creation, social media management and community engagement within an established international non-profit organisation. The opportunity to develop a professional portfolio, build your network and contribute to initiatives that support and connect women across Barcelona.

If you or someone you know is interested, please contact us here: [bwn@bcnwomensnetwork.com](mailto:bwn@bcnwomensnetwork.com)
