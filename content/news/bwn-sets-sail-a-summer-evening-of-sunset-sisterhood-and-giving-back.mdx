Barcelona Women's Network kicked off the summer season in style with a sunset sailing evening at Port Olímpic, bringing members and guests together for an evening on the water filled with ocean breeze, drinks, and pica pica.

Beyond the fun, the event carried real purpose: every ticket sold went toward supporting BWN's charitable causes, turning a relaxed evening at sea into a meaningful contribution to the community.

The event was organized by Tana Sabatino, with support from the BWN board, including President Lyn Lozano Galicia, Anuradha Mitra Ghemawat, Shima Khezr Carroll, Elisabeth Cardis, and Gwen Perry, whose continued dedication keeps moments like this possible. BWN extended thanks to all who attended, including Andrea Ramírez, for helping make the evening a success.

For BWN, evenings like this capture what the network is all about: connection, community, and giving back, all at once. And there's more to come! The network has a full slate of summer events planned for members and newcomers alike.

Those curious about joining are welcome to attend an upcoming event to meet the community firsthand. More information is available at [bcnwomensnetwork.com](http://bcnwomensnetwork.com) or by reaching out directly to BWN.
