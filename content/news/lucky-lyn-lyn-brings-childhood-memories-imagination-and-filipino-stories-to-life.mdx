**Barcelona, Spain — September 26, 2026** — Stories, imagination and the magic of childhood took center stage as *Lucky Lyn-Lyn*, a new children's book series by Filipina author and entrepreneur Lyn Lozano Galicia, celebrated its book launch in Barcelona, Spain.

Held at BHS on Carrer de Joan d'Àustria, the family-oriented celebration marked an exciting milestone for a literary project inspired by the author's own childhood adventures in the Philippines. The series brings to life the little experiences, friendships and discoveries that helped shape the author, transforming cherished memories into stories for a new generation of young readers.

Designed to appeal especially to children ages 3 to 10, *Lucky Lyn-Lyn* celebrates curiosity, imagination, courage, kindness, family and the importance of believing in oneself. Its stories encourage young readers to discover the extraordinary in everyday moments and to recognize that even the simplest experiences can become meaningful adventures.

## From Childhood Memories to Stories for Children Everywhere

For Lyn Lozano Galicia, *Lucky Lyn-Lyn* is more than a collection of children's books. It is a way of sharing a piece of her childhood and Filipino heritage with children and families around the world.

Originally from the Philippines and now based in Barcelona, Galicia draws inspiration from real-life childhood memories, bringing Filipino settings, traditions and everyday experiences into stories that can resonate with readers across cultures.

Through the adventures of Lyn-Lyn and her loyal companion, Bing-Bing, the series introduces young readers to lessons about friendship, honesty, generosity, teamwork and courage. Each story offers children an opportunity to imagine, explore and learn while enjoying the simple pleasures of being young.

The series also reflects Galicia's desire to preserve the spirit of childhood—the excitement of discovering something new, the confidence to try an idea, and the friendships that make life's adventures even more meaningful.

## A Celebration for Families and Young Readers

The September 26 launch was planned as a fun-filled, family-friendly gathering, welcoming friends, supporters, families and children to discover the world of *Lucky Lyn-Lyn*.

With its emphasis on storytelling and imagination, the event celebrated not only the arrival of a new children's book series but also the beginning of a broader journey to bring these stories to readers internationally.

For Galicia, sharing these books with children is an opportunity to remind them that their own experiences, ideas and dreams matter. Whether discovering a new adventure, learning to be brave or finding joy in an ordinary day, every child has a story worth celebrating.

## A Mission That Reaches Beyond the Books

Alongside its literary ambitions, *Lucky Lyn-Lyn* carries a charitable purpose. A portion of the series' proceeds is intended to support an orphanage in the Philippines, connecting the joy of reading with a commitment to helping children in need.

This charitable dimension reflects the heart of the project: inspiring children to dream big, embrace kindness and believe in the difference they can make in the world.

Galicia hopes to expand the series' reach through international publication while also bringing the books to readers in the Philippines, where the stories' cultural roots lie.

As *Lucky Lyn-Lyn* begins its journey from personal childhood memories to books shared with families across borders, its message remains simple: imagination opens doors, kindness brings people together, and the little moments in life can lead to the biggest adventures.

**About Lyn Lozano Galicia**

Lyn Lozano Galicia is a Filipina author and entrepreneur based in Barcelona, Spain. Inspired by her childhood adventures in the Philippines, she created the *Lucky Lyn-Lyn* children's book series to encourage children everywhere to dream, be brave, choose kindness and believe in themselves.
